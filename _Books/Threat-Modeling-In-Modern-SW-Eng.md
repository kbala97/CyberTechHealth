title:  "Threat Modeling in Modern Software Engineering"
---
Excellent book that expands the foundations of continuous threat modeling while addressing the growing impact of LLMs and AI. It walks through multiple methodologies with practical, real‑world use cases, making the concepts easy to apply regardless of your environment.
The author does a great job explaining advanced topics such as risk and controls, impact of cloud and microservices architectures, and how threat modeling integrates into modern CI/CD pipelines. A solid, forward‑looking read for anyone building or maturing an AppSec or product‑security program.
